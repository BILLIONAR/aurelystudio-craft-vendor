AurelyStudio — Craft Fair Planner · Maker Business
Version 3.0.0

OPEN YOUR APP
https://billionar.github.io/aurelystudio-craft-vendor/

On iPhone or iPad, open the link in Safari. To install, use Share > Add to Home Screen. Desktop and Android browsers also support installation. This is the full app, with no demo limits.

CONNECT YOUR DEVICES ONCE
On your first device, open Device Sync > Create my private workspace. On the other device, use the private link or code shown under Connect another device. Connect another device can copy a private link or show a QR code. Keep the code private: anyone with it can read and change your synced workspace.

Saved changes sync automatically while the app is open and online. The other device checks about every 20 seconds and when it becomes visible. Check for Synced before closing the app. Offline changes stay saved locally and sync after reconnection. If both devices change the same record, the app asks which details to keep.

Safari and a Home Screen app may use separate local storage. Connect each browser or installed app you use. Keep a separate copy of your code; business backups do not include it.

YOUR PRODUCTS
Inventory accepts your own main categories, subcategories and item types. Beadable and Carabiner are suggestions, not restrictions. Add keychains, bag charms, bookmarks or any other products later. Each stocked item has its own count, selling price, cost to make and notes. Add made stock when new items are finished.

YOUR ORDERS
Save a customer, order date, due date, source, status and multiple items. Sources include Online, Word of Mouth, Local Shop and Craft Show. Add item details and making notes. Custom items can be ordered without deducting inventory.

Open orders hold stocked items: available stock decreases while physical stock stays unchanged. Mark an order Delivered when fulfilled: the app creates one linked sale and deducts stock once. Additional payments do not create another sale. Delivered item details remain history; record returns through the linked sale.

PAYMENTS
Record Zelle, Venmo, Cash and other methods, including deposits and later payments. See total, paid and remaining. The app records payments; it does not send, collect or request money. Receipts use recorded payments. Profit uses delivered sales and item costs. These are planning records, not tax accounting.

YOUR FAIR TOOLS
Events, applications, booth planning, packing, stock history, returns, expenses, goals, reports and market tools remain available. Exact dated records remain in history across years.

PRINT AND PERSONALIZE
Print Studio includes Order book, Inventory list, price cards, care cards, shop QR signs and market reports. Choose A4 or Letter in the app and browser print dialog. Print or save as PDF. Scan a printed QR proof before using it.

Theme Studio saves palettes, independent main/accent/background colors, interface and heading fonts, five handwriting accents, Night Mode and Extra Calm Mode. Forms and money stay readable. The official AurelyStudio logo remains unchanged.

BACKUPS
Use Backup & Data > Export backup regularly and before clearing browser storage. Import replaces the workspace after confirmation, then syncs restored records if connected. Older AurelyStudio v1/v2 backups are supported; old free-text orders remain historical records. Backups from another seller require a separate compatibility check.

Open through HTTPS or a local web server. Double-clicking index.html is not a supported mobile/sync installation method. Included font and QR library licenses remain in fonts/ and vendor/. Application identity and ownership: AurelyStudio.
